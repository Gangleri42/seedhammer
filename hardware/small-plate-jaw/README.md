# Small plate jaw

A printable adapter that holds the small 85 x 55 mm plate in the
machine's clamp, which is sized for the square 85 x 85 mm format.
Print it before cutting your first small plate; every flow that offers
the small format assumes the plate sits fixed.

- [`small-plate-jaw.3mf`](small-plate-jaw.3mf) — ready to slice.
- [`small-plate-jaw.step`](small-plate-jaw.step) — the CAD solid, for
  editing or exporting to another format.
